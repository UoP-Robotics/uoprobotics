About Us

Who are we

The student robotics group UoP Robotics operates in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering of the University of Peloponnese in the city of Patras.The team consists of 32 undergraduate students from both Mechanical Engineering and ECE departments with the aim of implementing innovative ideas and applications in the field of technology and in particular in the field of robotics.The team is engaged in the development of an Unmanned Ground Vehicle (UGV) equipped with an arm, with the aim of operating it in scenarios of Environmental Control, Search & Rescue, Industry, and Agriculture, utilizing cutting-edge technologies. Additionally, the team designs STEM-based robots in the categories of Line-following, Maze Solving, and Sumo. The scientific supervisor of the team is Mr. Sotiris Christodoulou.

FAQs

The team is open to anyone from both the Electrical & Computer Engineering and Mechanical Engineering departments with the same passion for technology and robotics like us.

The team develops Unmanned Ground Vehicles (UGVs) equipped with robotic arms for application in industry, agriculture, environmental control and search & rescue operations. Additionally, we design STEM-based robots, including line followers, maze solvers and sumo robots.

The team operates within the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Peloponnese in the city of Patras, Greece
