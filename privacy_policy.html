Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The UoP Robotics Team of the University of the Peloponnese respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with both the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679 and the Greek Law 4624/2019.

1. Data Controller

Our team is responsible for collecting and processing your data. For more information and any questions you may have regarding this policy, you can contact us via email at roboticsteam@go.uop.gr.

2. Data we collect

When you fill out and submit the contact form, our website creates a pre-filled email in your local mail client. By sending this email, we receive and process the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • The subject and the content of your message.

In case you don't want to contact with us through our contact form, you can also find us on social media:

  • Instagram: uop.robotics
  • LinkedIn: uoprobotics
  • TikTok: @uop.robotics
  • Facebook: Robotics Team UoP

3. Cookies 🍪

Our website is designed to be as most privacy-friendly as possible. Therefore, we don't use Cookies, trackers, or any similar technologies to monitor your browsing behavior, analyze traffic or collect any personally identifiable information.

4. Purpose & Legal Basis for processing

We collect and process your data exclusively to respond to your messages, questions, feeback or suggestions sent via email. The legal basis for this processing is your consent and our legitimate interest in communicating with users who reach out to us.

5. Data Retention

Your data is stored strictly within our team's official email inbox (roboticsteam@go.uop.gr) only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose of our communication (e.g., to resolve your query). Once the communication is concluded, your email and personal data are deleted unless retention is required by law or university regulations.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • The right to access and receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request the correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • The right to request the erasure of your data ("the right to be forgotten").
  • The right to withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at roboticsteam@go.uop.gr.

7. Right to Complain

If you believe that our processing of your personal data violates data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complain with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Kifissias 1-3, P.C. 115 23, Athens, Greece, www.dpa.gr).
