Kenderis

The Maze Solver project developed by UoP Robotics at the University of the Peloponnese is an autonomous mobile robot designed to navigate and solve structured mazes without human intervention. Built around an Arduino-compatible microcontroller, the platform processes sensor data in real time, identifies walls and available paths, and controls the robot’s motion through the maze. Its modular architecture enables the integration of different sensors, actuators, and control strategies, making it a flexible system for experimentation and further development.

At the core of the project are maze-search and path-planning algorithms that allow the robot to make navigation decisions, detect dead ends, explore alternative routes, and progressively determine an efficient path toward the target. The system combines environmental perception, decision-making, motor control, and continuous position correction to achieve reliable movement under changing conditions. Depending on the maze configuration and the objectives of each experiment, different search techniques can be implemented and evaluated in terms of execution time, travelled distance, computational complexity, and navigation accuracy.

Beyond its competitive and technical capabilities, the Maze Solver serves as an educational robotics platform through which team members develop practical knowledge of embedded systems, autonomous navigation, algorithm design, sensor integration, and real-time programming. The project demonstrates how relatively accessible hardware can be combined with intelligent algorithms to create a complete autonomous robotic system, bridging theoretical concepts in computer science, electronics, and control engineering with real-world implementation.
